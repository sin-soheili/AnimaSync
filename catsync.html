Live — mic straight to a pop‑out window

—
No audio file needed — this reads the mic and drives the cat in realtime, in a window of its own you can hand to OBS as a window capture and chroma‑key. The amber tick on the meter is the silence threshold: your voice should cross it, your room shouldn't.
In that window – hides the bar and shrinks to just the cat, Fit sizes it 1:1, h toggles and Esc stops. Drag its edge to scale. Keep it somewhere visible — a browser stops drawing a window that's minimized or completely covered.
